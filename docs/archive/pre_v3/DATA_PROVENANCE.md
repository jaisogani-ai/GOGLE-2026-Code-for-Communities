## 1. The Core Provenance Invariant

> **"Every record of operational health data in this repository is SYNTHETIC or SIMULATED. Not one record describes a real patient, a confidential government consignment, or an unconsented physical facility."**

What is grounded in reality is the **taxonomic structure of failure modes, policy-based governance rules, and clinical multipliers**:
- **policy-based Rules**: Sourced from published General Financial Rules (GFR 2017 Rules 211, 213, 22), National Health Mission (NHM) Operational Guidelines, Drugs & Cosmetics Rules 1945, and the Epidemic Diseases Act 1897.
- **Clinical Surge Multipliers**: Sourced from published WHO and National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme (NVBDCP) outbreak clinical management protocols.
- **Data Shape**: Seeded deterministic pseudo-random generators reproducing the intermittent, zero-inflated demand patterns observed in rural public health networks.

## 2. Provenance Taxonomy Enforced in Code

Defined in `tathyon/schema.py` as the `Provenance` enum:

| Provenance Label | Scope & Usage | Code Enforced |
|---|---|:---:|
| `REAL_PUBLIC` | Published policy-based laws, CAG performance audit citations, WHO clinical guidance, and administrative boundaries. | Yes |
| `REAL_USER_PROVIDED` | Data explicitly uploaded or entered by an operator during interactive evaluation. | Yes |
| `SYNTHETIC` | Generated baseline ledger records, facility coordinates, and historical consumption time series produced by `tathyon/generator.py` and `tathyon/demo.py` under seed `42`. | Yes |
| `SIMULATED` | Forward-looking counterfactual trajectories generated by `tathyon/twin.py` and response plans generated by `tathyon/planner.py`. | Yes |
| `DERIVED` | Analytical outputs, 6-dimension health scores, holdout MASE evaluations, and risk rankings calculated from graph state. | Yes |
| `HYBRID` | Scenarios combining verified baseline models with injected synthetic shocks. | Yes |

## 3. Synthetic Data Tables & Generation Parameters

All core tables are generated reproducibly by `tathyon/demo.py` and `tathyon/generator.py`:
- `data/facilities.parquet`: 20 facilities across 4 administrative tiers (PHC, CHC, District Hospital, District Warehouse).
- `data/truth.parquet`: 48,000 ground truth rows (20 facilities $\times$ 10 SKUs $\times$ 240 days).
- `data/observed.parquet`: 48,000 ledger claim rows with injected real-world failure patterns (phantom inventory, expired lots, short delivery).
- `data/equipment.parquet`: 120 biomedical asset records tracking uptime claims vs physical attestation.

  "status": "READY_FOR_SYSTEM_OF_RECORD",
  "execution_boundary": "PAYLOAD_STAGED_NOT_EXECUTED_EXTERNALLY",
  "tathyon_provenance": "SIMULATION",
  "disclaimer": "EXECUTION BOUNDARY: PAYLOAD_STAGED_NOT_EXECUTED_EXTERNALLY. Tathyon is an intelligence and verification control plane that operates BESIDE policy-based systems of record (e-Aushadhi / DVDMS). Physical stock movement occurs only when imported into and executed by the upstream government portal."
}
