## Executive finding

`data/real/dvdms_ledger.csv` is generated by `tathyon/generate_real_pilot_data.py`, not an operational DVDMS/e-Aushadhi export. The generator simulates stock movement with Poisson/random draws and creates pseudo-attestation identities, inspection durations, physical counts, and P6-shaped labels. The file's SHA-256 is `8229d3e4c0343d4faedc25749f99996dbaab16c0be3f75e859d275f0ff02fcd8`, matching the previously recorded generated benchmark. Its 369 label-shaped rows are not human field attestations.

The Mac has ample resources for the repository's small tabular model, but capacity cannot cure provenance and label deficiencies. The only safe current outcome is a read-only audit and a data-owner handoff. Do not train this file or claim real-data metrics.

## Data-owner handoff checklist

1. Obtain written approval from the system/data owner naming the exact permitted purpose, fields, facilities, time period, retention, local storage location, and whether model training is allowed.
2. Receive the untouched CSV/TSV export locally from the authorized owner; record source-system/export version, retrieval timestamp, timezone, facility scope, date span, file size, and SHA-256. Keep original read-only and out of version control.
3. Obtain the signed/approved authorization record and a named accountable reviewer. Independently verify the issuer and audit/reference number; do not rely on manifest strings or CLI flags.
4. Collect genuine P6 physical-attestation records with unique attestation IDs linked to facility, resource, inspection time, claimed record, physical-present and usable counts, attester identity/role, custodian separation, evidence/source reference, and applicable P6 version. Reconcile every supervised label to its attestation record.
5. Resolve missing-field mapping before fitting. Missing real-data features must remain missing and be listed in `docs/real_data_features.md`; do not use the current default-zero/neutral substitutions as if observed.
6. Confirm sufficient labels across enough facilities and time for disjoint facility holdout plus a strictly later temporal test set, with both classes represented in train and test. If not, report `REAL DATA VALIDATED BUT LABELS INSUFFICIENT` and do not fit.
7. Correct and test the provenance-document checks, feature availability gate, split assertions, challenger acceptance branch, registry artifact digest, and registry-bound serving selection before an eligible training run.
8. Once these checks pass, train locally with the unchanged baseline hyperparameters, no upload, a single reproducible run, fixed seed, facility/time split, PR-AUC, fixed-budget hit rate, and same-budget random ablation. Register data/manifest/feature/code/split/seed/metrics/artifact hashes and approval evidence.
9. Have the data owner and model reviewer sign off separately on source authorization, label authenticity, performance, access controls, rollback, and whether serving is allowed.

## Important limitation

The current trainer has safety checks for this known generated file and the dry-run is read-only in the observed path. The broader pipeline is **not yet sufficient** to authenticate arbitrary future sources or attestation documents, preserve missing features as missing, or cryptographically bind a serving artifact to an approved registry entry. Those gaps must be fixed and tested before real training/serving. This audit did not modify the data, registry, card, quarantine files, or model artifact.
