TATHYON is a prototype for evidence-backed health-resource exception resolution. It demonstrates CSV-shaped intake, row quarantine, an advisory Trust Queue, synthetic map and allocation scenarios, and audit-oriented human review. The local resource ledger is generated simulation, real-data training is blocked pending authorized records and human-attested labels, and no live government or Google Maps integration is configured. Synthetic benchmark metrics are not field performance or patient impact. The product hypothesis is that an authorized export plus independent physical checks and accountable follow-up can help a health organization close stock discrepancies faster; this requires a real pilot to validate.
