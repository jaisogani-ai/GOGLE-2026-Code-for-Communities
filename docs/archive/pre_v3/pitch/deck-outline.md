# TATHYON — Honest Prototype Demo Deck

**Audience:** judges / design partners  
**Truth label:** Prototype with synthetic data. No live government integration, paid pilot, real-data model, patient outcome, or verified supply-chain impact has been demonstrated. Any application screenshot must retain the visible synthetic-demo banner.

## Slide 1 — The product question

- **Title:** Can a health team close stock exceptions with evidence?
- **Message:** When a stock record and a physical count disagree, the important workflow is to verify, document, assign, resolve and learn.
- **Visual:** Simple report → human count → exception → reviewed action → closure evidence. Label the example `SYNTHETIC DEMO`.

## Slide 2 — Why examine this workflow

- C-DAC describes DVDMS/e-Aushadhi as covering procurement, inventory and distribution; TATHYON must complement such systems rather than replace their records.
- NHM's 17th CRM and state CAG audits describe availability, freshness, coverage and physical-verification gaps in particular reviewed settings. Do not turn those observations into an India-wide prevalence claim.
- **Visual:** Evidence cards with geography/date and limits.

## Slide 3 — Product hypothesis

- Authorized stock export enters a strict parser.
- Bad rows are quarantined with reasons.
- Comparable evidence can prioritize a human check.
- A reviewer records the count, decision and closure trail.
- **Hypothesis to test:** This shortens time-to-resolution and improves verified usable availability for chosen items.

## Slide 4 — Working prototype today

- Local CSV/TSV-shaped intake and typed quarantine.
- Append-only in-memory event log and human attestation path.
- Deterministic Trust Queue and bounded optimization examples.
- Medicines/beds/personnel anomaly intake is currently a deterministic statistical prototype, not a trained AI model.
- **Visual:** Screenshots must show synthetic/demo, unverified, and unavailable states where relevant.

## Slide 5 — Synthetic scenario results (simulation only)

- If presenting the existing benchmark, show its seed, scenario assumptions, metrics, denominators and random/baseline comparison.
- Label every number: `SIMULATED BENCHMARK — NOT FIELD PERFORMANCE`.
- Do not say “days saved in clinics,” “real stockout reduction,” “AI proved,” “lives saved,” or “impact.”
- **Visual:** Reproducible benchmark chart from the checked report, with simulation label in the chart itself.

## Slide 6 — Where AI may help

- Potential: transcribe messy reports with source spans, explain a computed signal in plain language, and answer read-only questions over authorized records.
- Deterministic: validation, reconciliation arithmetic, P6 labeling, thresholds, optimization constraints and approvals.
- Current limit: live Gemini was not verified in the checked environment; role definitions/tests do not mean six trained or production-connected agents.

## Slide 7 — Map experience

- 2D map/list of generated sample facilities; selecting a marker opens its matching demo detail.
- Example routes are not road-routed, approved, or executable.
- Google Maps is an optional provider requiring the owner-configured restricted browser key and Map ID. Current local environment had neither; no Google rendering was verified.
- **Visual:** Persistent synthetic-data legend; no `LIVE GRID` label.

## Slide 8 — Human authority and audit

- Recommendations are advisory. No displayed proposal should be described as dispatched or executed.
- Physical facts require authenticated, independent human evidence under the applicable local process.
- Hash chaining is tamper-evident within the prototype; it is not a production-grade identity, signature or external anchoring system.
- Do not describe the TATHYON reconciliation report as a statutory GFR form or certificate.

## Slide 9 — First pilot design to validate

- One willing organization, small facility cohort, one selected commodity class, authorized exports, independent physical checks, and a human-owned closure process.
- Shadow mode first; do not write to a system of record or move stock.
- Measures: source coverage/freshness, verification yield at a fixed visit budget vs same-budget random, time to evidence-backed closure, verified usable availability and expiry exposure.
- Stop if permissions, identifier mapping, independent checks or comparator data are unavailable.

## Slide 10 — Buyer and go-to-market unknowns

- Possible operator: facility/district logistics or pharmacy lead; confirm through interviews.
- Possible buyer: state medical-services/procurement body, State Health Society, district administration or implementation partner. No payer or budget has been confirmed.
- A private hospital network may offer a faster workflow test, but it does not validate government procurement or public-sector fit.
- **Ask:** Who owns the unresolved exception today, what action can they authorize, and what line item could pay for closure?

## Slide 11 — What we will not claim

- No authorized e-Aushadhi/DVDMS API connection or live government system-of-record write.
- No verified real-data supervised model: current `data/real/dvdms_ledger.csv` is generator output and training is blocked.
- No live Gemini or Google Maps session verified here.
- No real facilities/resources/routes represented by the synthetic map.
- Federation is a future design with a synthetic demo, not real BRICS participation or privacy-preserving production training.
