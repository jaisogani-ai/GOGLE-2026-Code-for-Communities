# TATHYON Mac Real-Data Pipeline & Training Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-09-30  
**Workspace:** `/Users/jaisogani/Downloads/tathyon 2`  
**Decision:** **No verified real-data supervised model has been trained. Do not train on the current `data/real/dvdms_ledger.csv`.**

## Executive result

The local Mac has sufficient resources for the current tabular model pipeline (the pipeline reports 10 CPU cores and 16 GB RAM). That does not make the currently available dataset suitable for real-data training. The file at `data/real/dvdms_ledger.csv` is generated by `tathyon/generate_real_pilot_data.py`, which simulates stock movement and pseudo-random discrepancy events. Its 369 apparent labels are generated by code, not collected as human field attestations. The local dry run recognized the generator hash and correctly reported `FILE PRESENT BUT UNVERIFIED` and blocked serving it as a real model.

Therefore, TATHYON has previously fitted models on simulated/toy data, but it has **not** been proven trained on authorized real operational stock data with valid human-attested outcomes. The current dry-run command did not fit a model. Training on the present file would create more simulation results, not a real pilot model, so no such fit was run.

## Controls added during this audit

1. The trainer blocks this file's known generated SHA-256 before fit or registry/artifact writes.
2. Non-dry-run training requires a provenance manifest linked to the exact source hash and attestation/authorization records. This validates declarations and hashes only; it does not authenticate the issuing organization or signer.
3. Dry-run avoids pipeline artifact, registry, data-card, exclusion-summary, and quarantine writes.
4. Registry history now has zero approved real models; toy fit records remain marked revoked or benchmark-only.
5. Map/API provenance now labels resource-state examples and generated routes as `SYNTHETIC_DEMO`; route objects explicitly mark their geometry as not routed. The UI carries the demo label even after provider load. Google Maps credentials are session-only and the Advanced Markers provider requires a Map ID.
6. Added a report-intake anomaly review path for medicines, beds, and personnel. It uses a deterministic robust deviation signal, abstains when comparable history is insufficient, stores source observations as unverified, and writes advisory events only. This is not an AI model, does not prove cause or fraud, and does not change verified stock or create training labels.

## Remaining safety requirements before real pilot training

1. A data owner must independently verify authorization, signer identity, attestation records, source system, and permitted use. Do not treat manifest booleans as proof.
2. Confirm serving paths enforce provenance, model approval, and matching data/model hashes; the audit does not claim a complete production deployment review.
3. Rename/document `tathyon/train_ai_roles.py` as a deterministic role calibration/evaluation harness. It is not LLM training and should not be presented as real-data model training.
4. Keep map and Gemini provider status separate from local test status; expose live/fixture/unavailable state explicitly.
5. Configure department/state authorization and verification procedures with the pilot owner before field use.

## Legal and document-credibility correction

The previous live UI called its output a statutory “GFR Form 24” medicine-stock certificate, imitated an official Chhattisgarh header, claimed a CMO digital signature, and cited GFR Rules 213 and 214 together. That was unsupported: the GFR materials reviewed describe Rule 213 physical verification and Rule 214 buffer stock; Form 24 was not established as a medicine-stock certificate. The endpoint/UI now call this a TATHYON reconciliation report, explicitly disclaim government status/signature, and describe expiry risk as advisory. The old endpoint remains only as a compatibility alias. Approval and attestation copy now points to TATHYON policy and applicable state/department procedures instead of asserting an unverified Rule 22 delegation.

## Deep-research product direction

The strongest problem worth validating is not “another inventory dashboard.” It is the operational gap between **what an LMIS reports, what is physically usable at a facility, and whether a responsible officer can close the resulting exception**. An initial product wedge should focus on reconciling an authorized stock export with an independently verified count, managing the exception evidence and corrective follow-up, and measuring resolution time/availability impact. This is a research hypothesis, not proof of buyer demand or permission to integrate.

NHM’s 17th Common Review Mission reports e-Aushadhi use and also describes facility variation, stock-outs, and inconsistent physical verification/forecasting. CAG’s Punjab performance audit reports continued availability/procurement issues despite an e-Aushadhi deployment. UNICEF describes fragmented information systems and weak data use as recurring health-supply-chain barriers. A Zambia public-hospital study reports differences between physical counts and electronic LMIS records; this is analogous evidence, not direct evidence about India. [NHM 17th CRM](https://nhm.gov.in/New-Update-2024-26/CRM/17th-CRM-2025.pdf), [CAG Punjab audit](https://cag.gov.in/ag2/punjab/en/audit-report/details/121818), [UNICEF supply-chain barriers](https://www.unicef.org/supply/media/20346/file/Supply-chains-save-lives.pdf), [Zambia LMIS study](https://doi.org/10.4236/pp.2025.162005)

The Ministry of Finance’s GFR 2017 places physical verification and buffer stock in separate rules: Rule 213 covers physical verification; Rule 214 concerns buffer stock. Any TATHYON claim about GFR Form 24, statutory requirements, independent attesters, or specific approval authority must be checked against the applicable current rule and state procedure before appearing as legal fact. [Department of Expenditure GFR compilation](https://doe.gov.in/files/circulars_document/Compilation_of_amendments_in_GFR_2017_English_upto_31_12_2023_Final.pdf)

YC company pages and Reddit threads are useful for competitor discovery and anecdotal user pain, not proof of government purchasing, clinical impact, or Indian public-sector willingness to pay. The prompt pack below requires that distinction and asks Antigravity to attack, rather than assume, the product wedge.

## Remaining real-world requirements

The project needs an authorized government or facility data provider, permitted local handling terms, genuine source exports, and a documented independent-attestation process before the real-data trainer can be used for an operational model. Until then, use the pipeline in dry-run/audit mode and present all benchmark data/results as simulated.
