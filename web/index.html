CSV/TSV with FacilityCode, DrugCode, AvailableStock, IssuedQty, Date (optional: DrugName, period_days, BatchNo, ExpiryDate, VED, cold_chain). Rows stay UNVERIFIED until counted.

3 · Sample dataset (optional, for demonstration)

No government stock, bed or attendance feed is connected. To try the full loop, load realistic SAMPLE stock, prior counts, bed occupancy and staff attendance for the real facilities of one district. Every sample value is badged SAMPLE.
